Process

Five steps, from brief to handover

  1. 01

    Brief

    We start with how you use the space, what you already own and what you want to change. You leave with a clear scope.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We draw the layout, then choose materials and lighting. Nothing is ordered until you have approved the drawings.

  3. 03

    Build

    Walls, floors and joinery go in. We coordinate the trades and keep you up to date.

  4. 04

    Style

    Furniture, lamps and the smaller pieces arrive and are put in place.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We walk through the finished space with you and note anything to adjust.
