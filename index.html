Process

Five steps, from brief to handover

  1. 01

    Storytelling

    You tell us how you want to live or work. We listen, visit the space and shape the brief.

    1 to 3 weeks

    Timelines are a guide and vary with the scope of your project.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Your story becomes layouts, 3D visuals and a material selection, refined until it’s right.

    3 to 4 weeks

    Timelines are a guide and vary with the scope of your project.

  3. 03

    Build

    Site works and millwork begin. We coordinate the build and keep you updated.

    1 to 8 weeks

    Timelines are a guide and vary with the scope of your project.

  4. 04

    Furnishing

    Furniture, curtains and finishing pieces are delivered and placed.

    Within 1 week

    Timelines are a guide and vary with the scope of your project.

  5. 05

    Walkthrough and handover

    We walk the space with you, check every detail and hand over the keys.

    Timelines are a guide and vary with the scope of your project.

About

Born in Singapore. Built around you.

C Studio Interior designs and builds homes and commercial spaces. We begin with your story and stay with it through every drawing, every detail and every day on site.

Our founder brings 7 years in the industry, including millwork development for high-end luxury projects, and has completed multiple projects across Southeast Asia and in the US, from the shop drawing stage through to store opening.

Privacy note

The enquiry form collects your name, email address, phone number if you give one, project type, budget range if you give one, your message, and any look and choices you made on the material board. If you ask the material board to render a room, the options you chose (no personal details) are sent to an image service to make the picture. We use them only to reply to your enquiry.
